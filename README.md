# NUS Cheatsheets

I created this repository because I hate the evil Studocu and wanted to share the cheatsheets I painstakingly put together, for free :) They're carefully laid out for my use, and about as idiot-proof as I could make them.[^1]

After copious amounts of R&D investment (my printer card money), I also figured out the smallest page margins I could use without the printer cutting off any content. Tested on the RVRC printer. I can't vouch for other school printers, so your mileage may vary.

[^1]: Before any future employer raises an eyebrow: these are for NUS exams that explicitly allow notes. "Cheatsheet" just means a compact summary of formulas, concepts, and reminders. Also, idiot-proof doesn't mean error-proof. Double-check anything that looks off; I'm not responsible for misinformation or exam-day surprises :)
